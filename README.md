# CS2 Portfolio Manager

Track what your Counter-Strike 2 inventory is worth. See every item's Steam Market and CSFloat price, how prices have moved, and what's inside your storage units, all in one Android app.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/HAK978/de_portfolio/releases/latest)**

## Features

- **Portfolio value**: your inventory's total worth at a glance, with Steam Market and CSFloat totals side by side
- **Price changes**: 24-hour, 7-day and 30-day moves, plus a Top Movers list you can filter by price and item type
- **Price charts**: price history for each item, with zoom and pan
- **Inventory**: search, sort and filter by rarity, wear, type and collection
- **Storage units**: see what's inside each one and what it's worth
- **Search**: look up any CS2 item, check its prices, and compare items side by side
- **Case info**: see which cases still drop and what's inside them
- **Prices stay fresh**: they refresh automatically in the background

## Install

1. On your phone, open the [latest release](https://github.com/HAK978/de_portfolio/releases/latest) and download `app-release.apk`.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
3. Play Protect may warn that the app didn't come from the Play Store. Tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.

## Getting started

1. Tap **Sign in with Steam**. You sign in on Steam's own page, so the app never sees your password.
2. Optional: add your CSFloat API key in **Settings** to see CSFloat prices (csfloat.com → Profile → Developer).
3. Pull down on the inventory to refresh it any time.

## Updating

Open **Settings → About & updates → Check for updates**. The app downloads the new version and opens the installer.
